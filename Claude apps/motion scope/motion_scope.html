Magnification

Only motion whose rhythm falls between the two cutoffs gets amplified. Slow sway ≈ low Hz, wingbeats/vibration ≈ high Hz.

Noise

Lower Color removes the coloured speckle by driving motion from brightness. Smoothing blurs only the amplified motion to cut grain — raise it if noise is heavy, drop to 0 to keep the finest detail.

Quality

Lower detail = smoother, less noise, faster. Reset the baseline after the camera or scene settles.

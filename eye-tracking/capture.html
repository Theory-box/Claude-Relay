Guided capture

One continuous eye-tracking session

The tool talks you through it. Keep your eyes on the target the whole time; it tells you when to hold still, turn your head, lean, and slide the laptop. Runs on your device — nothing is sent until you upload the result.

  • Sit ~50–60 cm away, camera near eye level, face lit from the front
  • Whole run is about 80 seconds
  • Output is one small landmark file (plus an optional raw video)
  • For the raw-video test: bright, even light on your face works best — dim rooms add sensor grain that hides the fine detail
